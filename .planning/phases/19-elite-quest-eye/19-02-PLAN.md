<objective>
The hint for the quest and the safety tools (QST-03, QST-04). After the eye's click opens Forever's Listing tab (Plan 01), a small dialog-style box of ours shows what the player needs to list a group for that quest (name to type, Quests & Zones, zone), with our outline on the Quests & Zones category button; it clears when the finder closes, the tab changes, a listing is made, the close button is pressed or combat starts. Nothing of Blizzard's finder is selected, filled or written. Also `/tgf quest` (a debug command shipped alongside, never a gate) and the CLAUDE.md entries.

Geometry decision (CONTEXT asks the planner to check Forever's Listing layout): `LFGParentFrame` is 458x535; the Listing tab's top band (`RolesSection`, y -25 to -127) holds the solo / group role buttons the player needs, the category buttons start at y -132 (`CategoryView` TOPLEFT -124, first button at -8), and the Activity view spans the full width from y -125. There is no free room above the categories, so per the decision's fallback the finder is widened while the box shows ("like Browse") and the box sits in the added strip at the right of the roles band, above the categories, covering no control.

Purpose: the player gets from the eye to a posted group without the addon touching the secure description box or the restricted CreateListing.
Output: `QuestButton.lua`, `UI/InPlace.lua`, `Core.lua`, `CLAUDE.md`.
</objective>
