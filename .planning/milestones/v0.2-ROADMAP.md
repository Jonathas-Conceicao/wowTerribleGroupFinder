  1. The finder shows no GS column, no GS sort, no "Your GS" and no gear score tooltip lines, in game and in the preview
  2. Loading the addon with a v0.1 saved-variables file (with `gs` data) clears that data on first load, with no Lua errors, and nothing inspects group members after a ready check
  3. A grep for `ns.GS`, `GEAR_SCORE`, `GS(`, `row.gs` and `myGS` comes back clean, and `GearScore.lua` is gone from the repo and the TOC; the design notes stay in `research/GEARSCORE.md`
  4. The settings registry exists with defaults for all seven flat keys (item level x4, Dynamic refresh, swipe animations, region flags), each on, and a fresh or migrated DB reads them correctly

**Plans**: 2 plans

- [x] 06-02-PLAN.md — Schema-2 migration and the `ns.Settings` registry (seven keys, all on), with harness checks

**Notes**:

- Schema bump to 2 with `Migrate(db)` before every module `Init`; mutate the DB in place, never rebind `TerribleGroupFinderDB` (the Settings API holds a reference); the prototype's old `autoRefresh` key is cleared (never shipped)

### Phase 7: Item Level Spots

**Goal**: Players see item level, from the game's own figures, wherever they look at gear or at a player
**Depends on**: Phase 6
**Requirements**: ILV-01, ILV-02, ILV-03, ILV-04, ILV-05
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. Hovering a friendly player shows "Item Level N" on their tooltip once known (a quiet inspect fills it in); enemies and attackable players never get a line
  2. Weapon and armor tooltips (bags, character sheet, chat links, merchants, loot, comparison tooltips) show "Item Level N" in gold right under the item name, exactly once, including after a few seconds of hovering
  3. The character sheet's General stats show an Item Level row directly under Movement Speed, with Blizzard's own tooltip, and no taint or action-blocked errors in or out of combat
  4. The Inspect window shows the inspected player's item level as text at its top right, also on the first inspect after login
  5. The addon's quiet inspects never blank or disturb the player's own Inspect window, and a value never shows for another player

**Plans**: 3 plans

Plans:

- [x] 07-01-PLAN.md — Item level data split, quiet inspect guard, player tooltip spot (ILV-01, ILV-05)
- [x] 07-02-PLAN.md — Inspect window text and character sheet Item Level row, plus the PROJECT.md exception (ILV-03, ILV-04)
- [x] 07-03-PLAN.md — Item tooltip line, plus the CLAUDE.md Architecture lines (ILV-02)

**UI hint**: yes
**Notes**:

- Order inside the phase: player tooltip (moved from the prototype), Inspect text, character sheet row (Option A: hook `CharacterStatsPaneScrollBox.UpdateStats`, insert after `MOVESPEED`; Option B fallback only if the in-game check shows taint), item tooltip last (highest uncertainty). Each spot is an independent, pcall-guarded installer in `ItemLevelUI.lua`, hooks installed unconditionally and gated inside by the setting
- Item tooltip (from backlog 999.2): "Item Level N" in gold (`ITEM_LEVEL`, `NORMAL_FONT_COLOR`) under the name, before binding; level from `C_Item.GetDetailedItemLevelInfo`; use the ItemName line post-call, skip when a stock type-31 line exists, weapons and armor above level 1 only (retail look; skip items without a level); cover bags, character sheet, inspect, chat links (`ItemRefTooltip`), loot, vendor, auction house, compare tooltips; check `TOOLTIP_DATA_UPDATE` rebuilds never add it twice
- The sheet row is a deliberate, narrow exception to "never write fields on Blizzard frames" (writes the pane's `elementData`, calls its `SetDataProvider`); record it in PROJECT.md Key Decisions
- Quiet inspect guard: `InspectFrame.unit ~= nil`, a hook on `NotifyInspect` / `InspectUI` treating about 2 s as busy, GUID match at `INSPECT_READY` and at read time, never `ClearInspectPlayer()` if a later inspect started
- Gate: toggles for these spots land as registry keys here; the user-facing switches arrive in Phase 10
- In-game checks (run early): sheet row taint (research check 1: equip, scroll, switch Stats / Equipment / Titles / Pet, combat, `/reload` with the sheet open; `/console taintLog 2`), label text reads "Item Level" (2), item tooltip data and type-31 duplicates across inventory, bags, merchant, chat link, compare (3), Inspect size, anchor, first-inspect, hover-inspect then click, close during a quiet inspect (4)

### Phase 8: Region Flags and Row Icon Tooltips

**Goal**: Every listing shows where its player is, and every icon on a finder row says what it is when hovered
**Depends on**: Phase 6
**Requirements**: REG-01, REG-02, REG-03, REG-04, ICO-01, ICO-02
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. Each row shows a flag after the name: one US flag for US East and US West, an Oceania flag; a listing on an unknown server shows no flag
  2. The row tooltip names the region (US East, US West or Oceania)
  3. Your own listing shows the flag of its group leader's region (your own when you are solo or lead)
  4. After playing and adding Battle.net friends the known servers grow and survive `/reload`; a learned server never overrides a known one
  5. Hovering any row icon (flag, role icons, new player friendly, Discord, leader, friend / Battle.net friend / recent ally marks, friends in group) shows a tooltip saying what it is, while hover, click-select and right-click menu still work over the icons

### Phase 9: Dynamic Refresh

**Goal**: Refreshing the finder keeps the list on screen and updates it in place, with calm animations the author can also try in the preview
**Depends on**: Phase 6
**Requirements**: REF-01, REF-02, REF-03, REF-04
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. Pressing the finder's refresh button for the same category and activities keeps the list: new listings are sorted in, delisted ones are removed, the selection and whispered / invited marks stay, and a failed refresh leaves the list untouched
  2. Rows that leave swipe out and new rows swipe in at a visibly calmer speed than the prototype
  3. Rows below a removed row slide up into place instead of jumping, also when the list is scrolled
  4. In the preview, the refresh button simulates listings leaving and arriving with the same animations, and `/tgf reset` restores the sample data

**Plans**: 3 plans

Plans:

- [x] 09-01-PLAN.md — `UI/Motion.lua` (`ns.Motion`): calmer swipes, slide-up from recorded tops, resetter, click guard, `swipeAnimations` gate; Table / InPlace hand-off
- [x] 09-02-PLAN.md — Live Dynamic refresh: `dynamicRefresh` gate, cancel on new search, naming, CLAUDE.md taint / InPlace lines
- [x] 09-03-PLAN.md — Preview: reserve, `Preview.Churn` / `Preview.Refresh` through `Motion.Show`, both settings, `/tgf reset`

**UI hint**: yes
**Notes**:

- Extract `UI/Motion.lua` (`ns.Motion`) from `UI/Table.lua`: swipe out / in, slide-up that records each row's `GetTop()` by listing key before the update and animates from the old offset after it; `Motion.Show(previousKeys, selectedKey)` shared by the live path and the preview; named constants for swipe (about 0.3-0.4 s) and slide (about 0.3 s), tuned by feel; a new search cancels pending swipe state
- `OnSearch` gated by `dynamicRefresh` and `swipeAnimations` (independent: Dynamic refresh off = stock spinner and blank list; swipes off = list updates and rows jump)
- Pooled rows: reset (stop group, alpha 1, offsets) in the ScrollBox element resetter as well as in `FillRow`; never move anchors of pooled rows; no `UIFrameFade` or `OnUpdate` tweens
- Click guard while rows hold the old results during the swipe-out
- Preview: `Preview.Churn()` with reserve listings, the `IP.OnSearch` preview branch
- Known limit: `C_LFGList.Search` from a timer is blocked (`ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED`), so no refresh on a timer; Auto-refresh stays click or key press driven; the "Auto-refresh" icon is renamed Dynamic refresh
- Backlog 999.4 (quiet refresh and row swipes) lands here. Still-open cases from it: listings filtered out (search box, Needs my role) or in a collapsed section change without a swipe; test many changes at once, scrolled lists, the hovered row's tooltip, selection kept, marks kept, a failed refresh
- In-game checks: `SetDataProvider` / `SetItems` acquires frames synchronously, slide delta right when scrolled or sections collapsed, no stuck invisible rows after scrolling or a second refresh mid-swipe, no wrong-row animation after pooled reuse (research check 6)

### Phase 10: Settings Window

**Goal**: Players manage every optional extra in one native settings page and can jump from it to the Messages tab
**Depends on**: Phases 7, 8, 9 (the toggles control real behaviour)
**Requirements**: SET-01, SET-02, SET-03, SET-04, SET-05, SET-06, SET-07
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. The addon has a page under AddOns in the game's Settings, and `/tgf settings` opens it
