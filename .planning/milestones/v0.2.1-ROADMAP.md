## Phase Details

### Phase 12: Damage Meter Reset Prompt
**Goal**: Joining a group or entering an instance asks, with a clearly labelled prompt, whether to reset the damage meter, and each trigger can be turned off
**Depends on**: Nothing (built on `topic/dev`: `MeterReset.lua`, two settings, `/tgf meter`)
**Requirements**: MTR-01, MTR-02, MTR-03, MTR-04
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Joining a group with Details! loaded shows "[TGF] Reset Details! damage meter?" with Yes / No; Yes clears Details!, No leaves it
  2. With Details! not loaded and the game's meter on, the prompt names the damage meter and Yes clears the game's meter; with neither, nothing is asked
  3. Entering a dungeon, raid, battleground or other instance asks once; a login or `/reload` inside does not
  4. Turning either trigger off in Settings (Damage meter section) stops that prompt; `/tgf meter` shows the prompt at any time
**Plans**: 1 plan

Plans:
- [x] 12-01-PLAN.md — [TGF] prompt text (MTR-03), source check of triggers / settings / `/tgf meter` (MTR-01, 02, 04)

### Phase 13: Region Flags for Every Region
**Goal**: Every listing whose shard group is known shows its region, as a real flag where there is one and as two letters otherwise, with the game's full text in the tooltip
**Depends on**: Nothing
**Requirements**: REG-05, REG-06, REG-07, REG-08
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. US, AU, EU, CN, KR and TW listings show the author's flagpedia.net flags at one height, each at its own width, looking sharp on rows
  2. Groups such as `AU`, `EU`, `EU3`, `US2` show their country's flag and the tooltip names the full group
  3. A shard text that isn't two letters with an optional number (e.g. `EU Paris`) shows its first two letters as text on the row and the full text in the tooltip
  4. `Media/Source/flags.js` and the generated textures are gone; an internal note names flagpedia.net as the flags' source; the README doesn't mention it
**Plans**: 2 plans

Plans:
- [x] 13-01-PLAN.md — flag art: author's PNGs to Media/Source/flags, png2tga.js converter, six 64x32 TGAs, flags.js removed, SOURCE.md (REG-05, REG-08)
- [x] 13-02-PLAN.md — region rule: free-text groups, flags at own widths, two-letter text tag, preview cases, Settings / CLAUDE.md wording (REG-05, REG-06, REG-07, REG-08)

### Phase 14: Spec Points
**Goal**: A friendly player's tooltip shows their talent points per tree, from the game's own data, with a setting to turn it off
**Depends on**: Nothing (uses the item level quiet inspect)
**Requirements**: SPC-01, SPC-02
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Hovering a friendly player whose talents are known shows a line such as "36/12/3" under the item level line; your own tooltip shows yours
  2. Nothing shows while a player's talents are unknown, and the quiet inspect stays as guarded as for item level (no extra inspects, no interference with the Inspect window)
  3. The spec points option in Settings turns the line on and off on its own, independently of the item level options (on by default)
**Plans**: 2 plans

Plans:
- [x] 14-01-PLAN.md — talent points data in `ns.ILvl` (yours live, an inspected player's in the same guarded inspect answer, same GUID cache; stays away from Forever's inspected talent view; `/tgf ilvl` prints the figures)
- [x] 14-02-PLAN.md — "Talents 36/12/3" tooltip line after the item level line, the `specPoints` option ("Talent points", section "Item level and talents"), CLAUDE.md, HUMAN-UAT

**Note**: research moved the source to Forever's own talent frame figure (`C_Traits` group currency `spent` per tree); confirm in game with `/tgf ilvl` that it fills for inspected players (the probe also prints `GetSpecializationInfo(i, true)`'s `pointsSpent` for comparison)
