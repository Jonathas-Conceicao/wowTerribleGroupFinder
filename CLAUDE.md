# TerribleGroupFinder

WoW Forever addon (interface 16001, game type `camelot`): improves Forever's own group
finder (`Blizzard_GroupFinder_VanillaStyle`) **in place**: its Browse tab gets a sortable
table of solo players and groups. Early release (v0.3.0). License: GPL-3.0.

## Key Constraints
- **Forever only.** One TOC, `## Interface: 16001`. Retail is out of scope for now
- No flavour-forked Lua/XML files; if a flavour is added later, extend the `## Interface:` list
- **In place, not a new window.** In game the addon changes Forever's own finder; it never opens a window of its own. The stand-in window (`UI/MainFrame.lua`) is for preview and development only, used where the game's finder is missing, and mirrors its layout
- Forever runs on the modern client: guard reads with `issecretvalue()` where values may be secret, and never send addon messages during `C_ChatInfo.InChatMessagingLockdown()`
- Realms: Forever is one mega-realm per ruleset (PvP, PvE, RP-PvP, RP-PvE) and the group finder only lists players from your own, so whisper and invite by plain character name, without a realm suffix
- UI should look native to Forever (Blizzard templates and atlases), not a custom skin
- Taint: never replace or write fields on Blizzard's finder; only `Hide()` its parts (hooked to stay hidden), `HookScript`, `hooksecurefunc`, resize `LFGParentFrame`, and parent our own frames to it. Restricted calls (`Search`, `CreateListing`, `UpdateListing`) stay Blizzard's. Dynamic refresh never calls `C_LFGList.Search` (the game blocks it from a timer: `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED`); it only reads the answer of the game's own search differently. Outside the finder the addon only hooks (`hooksecurefunc`, `TooltipDataProcessor`) and adds its own frames / text; it never writes Blizzard's data. One accepted call: a late inspect answer rebuilds the shown player tooltip with `GameTooltip:RefreshData()` (out of combat, pcall-guarded) so its lines come in order. One more, the author's choice: the addon's Settings page's Move and Edit whisper messages buttons close the game's Settings panel through its own `Close` (`SP.CloseThen`); nothing is written on it. The one narrow exception to "in place" outside the finder: the character sheet's Item Level row, our own frame on the stats list's scroll target, which moves the shown stat rows' anchors to make room under Movement Speed (anchors only: nothing of Blizzard's stats data is written, because secure code reads it; an entry in that list taints the sheet). The other in-place spot outside the finder: the per-slot gear info, one frame of ours per slot button on the character sheet and the Inspect window (a child of the button, kept in a weak-keyed table, never a field on it, mouse-disabled), drawn from `hooksecurefunc` on `PaperDollItemSlotButton_Update` / `InspectPaperDollItemSlotButton_Update`; nothing of Blizzard's is written, no inspect started or cleared. The objective tracker is only post-hooked (`hooksecurefunc` on the Quest / Campaign module instances' `EndLayout`), our eye is our own plain Button parented to each block (never a field on the block, never `AddRightEdgeFrame`, never a secure frame: nothing in the tracker is protected), and the changes to Blizzard's tracker are anchors only, reset by the block's own layout: the block title's RIGHT anchor and, out of combat, the quest item button's (moved left of the eye). The eye's click is secure: a SecureActionButton of ours (a child of UIParent, placed over the hovered eye by screen coordinates, out of combat only, taken away when combat starts) runs a macro that `/click`s the game's `LFDMicroButton` (only while the finder is closed) and `LFGParentFrameTab1`, then two `SecureActionButtonTemplate` click-delegates of ours (`TGFQuestCategory`, `TGFQuestActivity`; their `clickbutton` set in PreClick, read only) that click the Quests & Zones category button and the zone row's checkbox: Blizzard's own handlers pick them as the player's clicks would, so `CreateListing` stays untainted; picking them from addon code would taint the Listing tab and block Post. The list is never scrolled or expanded; the description box takes no text from addons (`SetSecurityDisableSetText`, paste disabled). The quest hint is our own frame, a child of `LFGListingFrame` placed outside the finder beside the description box, or past its side tabs while that box isn't shown (the finder isn't resized for it), and our outline is parented to the Quests & Zones category button (read only)

## Forever's group finder model (keep the UI faithful to it)
- A listing is a solo player (with the roles they can play) or a group (leader + members). No applications, no applicant list
- Leaders browse solo players and invite them; solo players browse groups and whisper the leader. **No "apply"**: each row's button whispers (preset messages, Messages tab)
- Browse picks a category and activities (Blizzard's dropdowns), then searches; results are one list: your listing, then two collapsible sections (Solo players, Groups) with a divider each — groups first while solo, solo players first while leading
- Never remove a listing from view: hidden (until they relist), delisted and (with Needs my role) no-slot-for-you listings are greyed and moved to the end of their section. One exception (author's choice): a Dynamic refresh drops the delisted listings in its answer (they swipe out), and they stay out of later reads until the next new search or until relisted; a listing delisted since the last refresh still shows greyed until the next refresh
- Listing (create/update) stays Blizzard's own tab, untouched: its description box is secure. The one exception, kept on purpose: the delist button on your own row calls `C_LFGList.RemoveListing()`

## Architecture
- `Core.lua` — namespace, `TerribleGroupFinderDB`, `ns.Migrate` (schema 5: clears the obsolete gear score and auto-refresh keys on every load, in place, before every module Init; and once (schema below 3), for a player whose table already held any key at load, saves `meterResetOnGroup = true` so its new default (off) only reaches new installs; and once (schema below 4), for such a player, saves `true` for each of the six options on by default up to v0.2.2 (`ilvlPlayerTooltip`, `specPoints`, `ilvlItemTooltip`, `ilvlCharacterSheet`, `ilvlInspect`, `meterResetOnInstance`; a frozen list, never the registry) unless already saved as a boolean, so the new defaults (off) only reach new installs; and once (schema below 5), for such a player, saves `settingsSeen = "0.2.2"` (frozen: the last version before the Settings page's NEW tags) unless it holds a string; a table holding only `schema` counts as saved data), runtime state (`ns.state`), the questions the table asks (`ns.IsLeader/CanInvite/MyRoles/Party/MyListingSize/GroupSize`) answered from the game or the preview, `/tgf` (`preview`, `reset`, `version`, `ilvl`, `gear`, `region`, `quest`, `meter`, `settings` / `config`, `set <key> on|off`)
- `Settings.lua` — `ns.Settings`: the options registry (`Register`, `List`, `Init`, `Get`, `Set`, `OnChange`), flat boolean keys in `TerribleGroupFinderDB`, saved only once set (until then `Get` gives the default; other modules' keys are reserved), every option carries an explicit default; on only `eliteQuestButton`, `dynamicRefresh`, `swipeAnimations`, `regionFlags` (the addon only changes the group finder until the player opts in), the rest off (existing users keep the ones they had on through Migrate's schemas 3 and 4); keys in registration order: `eliteQuestButton`, `dynamicRefresh`, `swipeAnimations`, `regionFlags`, `ilvlPlayerTooltip`, `specPoints`, `ilvlItemTooltip`, `ilvlCharacterSheet`, `ilvlInspect`, `slotItemLevel`, `slotEnchants`, `meterResetOnGroup`, `meterResetOnInstance`; `slotItemLevel` and `slotEnchants` are read by `GearSlots.lua`; `eliteQuestButton` is read by `QuestButton.lua`; features read `Get` where they act (never cached); each option also carries `section`, `tooltip`, optional `parent`, optional `since` (the version it arrived in; `eliteQuestButton`, `slotItemLevel`, `slotEnchants` are `"0.3.0"`); `DB.settingsSeen` (a reserved key) is the version the player last saw the page in: `IsNew(key)` is true for an option whose `since` is newer (versions skipped add up), `MarkSeen()` saves the newest `since`, and `Init` saves the newest for a table without it (a new install sees no NEW tags); the page is `UI/SettingsPanel.lua`
- `Preview.lua` — sample categories, activities and listings (`ns.Preview`), same shape as `Listings.lua`'s (group member strings take an optional `@Zone`); `P.reserve` (8 Dungeons listings), `P.Churn` (1-3 listings leave, 1-3 arrive from the reserve), `P.DropDelisted` (shown delisted listings leave, as the game's refresh drops them), `P.DelistOne` (sometimes one turns delisted) and `P.Refresh` (the preview's Dynamic refresh through `ns.Motion.Show`, honouring both settings: delisted rows swipe out with the ones that left, and a listing may turn delisted a moment after; `dynamicRefresh` off shows a short spinner); `P.shardGroups` gives preview-only region groups (EU1, KR1, CN, TW1, EU Paris, BR1) to six samples, never saved; `/tgf reset` restores the samples and the reserve
- `ItemLevel.lua` — `ns.ILvl`: item level data. Yours from `GetAverageItemLevel`'s equipped figure, at least `C_PaperDollInfo.GetMinItemLevel` when the client has it, rounded down (as the character sheet); others' from `C_PaperDollInfo.GetInspectItemLevel` (a whole number; works on Forever after an inspect), else our average rounded; nothing while unknown or incomplete. The game's rule, undocumented, worked out from live samples (yours exact, nine players' inspects; one-handers, two-handers, wands, a bow, relics): 16 slots, the weapons as the better of the melee set (main hand + off hand, a two-hander twice) and the ranged set (ranged slot + off hand); inspect figures rounded. A session cache by GUID (a known value is never looked up again, only updated by a later inspect); the quiet inspect queue (one at a time, 1.5 s apart, out of combat, a 30 s wait after one that brings nothing) with its guard: never while the Inspect window is shown or pending, nor within 2 s of an inspect that is not ours (`NotifyInspect` / `InspectUnit` hooks) or while it awaits its answer (its `INSPECT_READY`, at most 4 s; no `ClearInspectPlayer()` meanwhile), values only for the GUID the latest inspect answered, `ClearInspectPlayer()` only when no later inspect started; `OnLearned` observers; `Inspected(unit)` for the Inspect window; talent points per tree (`TalentsOf(unit)`, `TalentsKnown(unit)`): yours live from your active talent config, others' from the inspect config read in the same guarded inspect answer before `ClearInspectPlayer()`, only when `C_Traits.HasValidInspectData()` says it is valid (invalid = unknown, a later inspect may fill it; talents count as a value for the 30 s wait), both as Forever's talent frame counts them (`C_Traits` group currency `spent`, trees in the game's order; your config the one whose type reads CamelotCombat, an unreadable type only when it is the only candidate), kept in the same GUID cache even when items don't load (`false` = answered, nothing readable; it and all zero count as known for 10 minutes, then re-read); nothing while unknown, secret or all zero; the guard also stays away while Forever's talent frame shows an inspected player (`PlayerSpellsFrame:IsInspecting()`); `/tgf ilvl` probe (prints every figure the client gives for you, checks our average against the game's, inspects each group member in turn and prints theirs, talent figures (C_Traits and `GetSpecializationInfo`'s `pointsSpent`, and one read after the clear; under one pcall), lists failed spot installers). Read-only exports `ILvl.SLOT_NAMES` and `ILvl.InspectReadyFor(unit)` (true for you, or when the latest inspect answered for that GUID). Session-only, no saved data
- `ItemLevelUI.lua` — `ns.ILvlUI`: the four item level spots, each an independent pcall-guarded installer gated by its `ns.Settings` key where it acts: friendly player tooltip (`ilvlPlayerTooltip`; enemies / attackable never; with a gold "Talents 36/12/3" line after the item level line, gated by its own `specPoints`, shown on its own when the item level line is off or unknown; nothing while unknown or all zero; both lines tracked, each added once per build; when an inspect answers while the tooltip shows that player, it is rebuilt with `GameTooltip:RefreshData()` (out of combat, never from inside our own rebuild) so both lines come in order, else they are added in order and updated in place; one quiet inspect fills both), item tooltips (`ilvlItemTooltip`; ItemName line post-call, gear above level 1, skipped when a stock item level line exists), the character sheet row (`ilvlCharacterSheet`; our own "Item Level:" row under Movement Speed, a frame of ours from Blizzard's stat row template on the stats list's scroll target, placed after every list update (`hooksecurefunc` on the ScrollBox's `Update`) by moving the shown rows' anchors to make room, the room taken from below at the top of the list and from above at its end in proportion to the scroll position (the list's scroll range doesn't count our row); the stripe follows the list; redrawn on the pane's OnShow, on gear changes and on the setting (and at install when the pane already shows), with its own tooltip (equipped and best owned); one more try at `PLAYER_LOGIN` when the pane isn't defined yet at our load; Blizzard's stats data is never touched), the Inspect window text (`ilvlInspect`; top right, on `Blizzard_InspectUI` load); failed installers are listed by `/tgf ilvl`
- `QuestButton.lua` — `ns.QuestButton`: the Elite quest eye (`eliteQuestButton`, read where it acts). An independent pcall-guarded installer (`ContinueOnAddOnLoaded("Blizzard_ObjectiveTracker")` when it isn't loaded) post-hooks `EndLayout` on the `QuestObjectiveTracker` and `CampaignQuestObjectiveTracker` instances and passes over `usedBlocks` (read only). Eligibility: `C_QuestLog.IsEliteQuest` (tag ID fallback, never the title text); none where `C_LFGList.CanCreateQuestGroup` gives the game's own eye, nor without `C_LFGInfo.CanPlayerUsePremadeGroup`; cached per quest, cleared on `QUEST_LOG_UPDATE`. Our own plain Button per block in a weak table (retail's 30x30 square button with the addon's purple eye icon, `tgf_icon_64x64`), at TOPRIGHT (5, 2) as retail places it, with the quest item button moved left of it and centred on it (out of combat; in combat a block with an item button waits for the end of combat); the header's RIGHT anchor moves only when the unbounded title still fits one line in the narrower width; tooltip "Find a group for this quest". The click is the secure cover's (see Key Constraints: `Q.Cover` on the eye's OnEnter, `Q.Recheck` after each layout, `Q.Uncover` on leave and at combat start): PreClick reads the quest and builds the macro (open the finder when closed, the Listing tab, then, without an active listing, the Quests & Zones category and the zone's activity), PostClick shows the hint and, with Quests & Zones picked, puts the cursor in the description box while it's empty (`SetFocus` only: `LFGListingComment.EditBox` refuses `SetText`, `Insert` and paste from addons, confirmed in game: "Call is illegal when disabled by security settings", and the try taints; no API sets the description, so the player types it). Confirmed in game (2026-11-25): addon code can post from a key press or click, `C_LFGList.CreateListing({ activityIDs = { id }, generalPlaystyle = n })` returns true and lists; `LfgListingCreateData` has no text field (unknown keys such as `comment` / `description` / `name` are ignored silently); the description always comes from the game's creation text fields, i.e. what the player typed in `LFGListingComment` (an addon-made post carries it; `GetActiveEntryInfo().comment` is then a secret value, `""` when empty); a `questID` is refused (false) for quests without `CanCreateQuestGroup` (where the Listing tab didn't open, `IP.ShowFinder` and the hint, nothing picked). The zone's activity (`Q.ActivityFor`): the Quests & Zones activity whose list name, full or short name equals the quest's zone (quest log header, then task quest zone, then quest UI map), ticked only when its row is on screen and not ticked yet. The hint: our dialog-style box (15 px inner padding), a child of `LFGListingFrame` just outside the finder beside the description box (re-placed when the Activity or Category view shows; past the side tabs while the box isn't shown), only a display: the quest name (gold, Elite tag) and Zone (with "(ticked)" once ticked; omitted when unknown), a close button, and our pulsing outline on the category button whose `categoryID` is 116 while it isn't picked; with an active listing it prints one chat line instead. It clears on finder close, tab change, a listing made and close. The eye's own click (no cover: in combat a chat line; the stand-in prints the hint as a chat line). `/tgf quest` prints the installer state (with the macro's `/click` targets found or missing), each tracked quest's elite / tag / game-eye / our-eye / zone answers and matched activity, and the Quests & Zones activities (and the failed steps). Every step of the eye's hover and click (layout recheck, cover build / place / removal, the cover's scripts, Arm, the delegates' PreClick, After, the description focus, hint, outline, the eye's own click) runs under a guard; Arm and both delegates clear `macrotext` / `clickbutton` first, so a failed step leaves a click that does nothing; the cover's pieces are built once (the named delegates are never created twice); a failure prints "Quest eye: <step> failed" once per step per session and keeps the error for `/tgf quest`
- `GearSlots.lua` — `ns.GearSlots`: per-slot item level and enchant beside each slot icon toward the model (left column right of the icon, right column left of it, 5 px from the icon; the weapons (main hand, off hand, ranged) above the icon, 4 px up), on the character sheet (unit player) and the Inspect window (`InspectFrame.unit`, only while `ns.ILvl.InspectReadyFor` is true and the item data has loaded; cleared on a new GUID and on OnHide), gated by `slotItemLevel` / `slotEnchants` (each on both windows; one line alone takes the first spot; `OnChange` redraws at once). `GS.Read(unit, slot)`: link and `C_Item.GetDetailedItemLevelInfo`, quality colour; enchant text from the tooltip line of type ItemEnchantmentPermanent with "Enchanted: " stripped, the item string's enchant ID, "Enchanted" when only the ID is there. The enchantable slots table: head, neck, back, chest, wrist, hands, legs, feet, main hand, any off-hand item, ranged only for bows / guns / crossbows (never the shoulders, whose enchants come only from a reputation, nor the belt, rings, trinkets, shirt or tabard; an enchant present on any slot still shows). "No enchant" in red at every level only for an enchantable, loaded, unenchanted item; pending data shows nothing: the game is asked to load it (`C_Item.RequestLoadItemDataByID`) and the window redraws on `GET_ITEM_INFO_RECEIVED` (one coalesced redraw a frame later, only for a window waiting on items), with three 1 s retries as a backstop; an inspect not yet answered has its own retry budget and `INSPECT_READY` redraws. No inspects of its own, no cache. Installers `slotsSheet` (one more try at `PLAYER_LOGIN`) and `slotsInspect` (on `Blizzard_InspectUI` load) through `ns.ILvlUI.Install`. `/tgf gear` probe: each slot's level, enchant ID, enchant line, `GetItemNumSockets`, enchantable; the inspected player's while the window shows; failed slot installers
- `Region.lua` — `ns.Region`: region flags and probe. A listing's `partyGUID` (`Party-<shard>-…`, never secret) carries the hidden shard its leader plays on, the same ID as the shard's realm ID; a shard's name gives its group: the trimmed free text between `CAMWOW` and `Realm` (`CAMWOW US2 Realm 8 PvP` -> US2, `CAMWOW EU Paris Realm 3 PvE` -> EU Paris; other names unknown). A group that is two letters plus an optional number (AU, EU, EU3, US2) shows its country's flag where we have art: US, AU, EU, CN, KR, TW (`Media/Textures/flag_<cc>`, the author's flagpedia.net PNGs converted by `Media/Source/png2tga.js`, drawn 10 px high at each flag's own width); any other group (EU Paris, a country without art) shows its first two letters as text in the flag's slot; an unknown shard shows nothing; the tooltip names the full group. Saved shards are free text (non-empty, up to 32 bytes, no control characters or `|`). `R.Flag(l)` returns the texture or `{ text }`. Preview-only groups come from `ns.Preview.shardGroups`. Only US and AU shards are seeded (seen live); other regions' are learned. Seeded IDs win; learned ones (your own shard via `GetRealmName`, Battle.net friends' via `C_BattleNet.GetFriendGameAccountInfo`, saved in `DB.shards`, sanitized on load) only fill gaps; a newly learned one redraws the table. `R.ForListing(l)` decides where a listing is: your own = the leader's region (yours when solo or leading; the listing's own shard when your leader flag can't be read). The flag (or, without art, the two-letter tag as text) follows the name on rows, drawn by `UI/Table.lua` through `R.Flag(l)`; the tooltip line names the group as the game does, "Region: US2" / "Leader's region: …" (where each group is, West or East, is not assumed). All gated by `regionFlags` (read where it acts; toggling redraws the table). `/tgf region` prints the probe (regions, realm IDs, GUID server IDs, friends' shards with the source that decided each, listings per shard)
- `MeterReset.lua` — `ns.MeterReset`: the damage meter reset prompt. Joining a group (not grouped → grouped, `GROUP_ROSTER_UPDATE`) and entering an instance (outside or another instance → this one, `PLAYER_ENTERING_WORLD`; the same instance again within 15 min of leaving it is the same run and doesn't ask; skipped when Details! is the meter and its own `segments_auto_erase` asks or erases, only read; a login or `/reload` only records where you are) each ask, gated by their own setting (both off for new installs; players with saved data keep them on through Migrate's schemas 3 (`meterResetOnGroup`) and 4 (`meterResetOnInstance`)); a trigger doesn't ask within 10 s of the last prompt shown or answered. The meter is Details! when loaded (`Details:ResetSegmentData()`), else the game's own meter only while its `damageMeterEnabled` CVar is on and `C_DamageMeter.IsDamageMeterAvailable()` (`C_DamageMeter.ResetAllCombatSessions()`); with neither nothing is asked. The dialog is our own frame (`DialogBorderTemplate`, the addon's icon on the left, Yes / No in the game menu's button style (`SharedButtonTemplate`, the panel button where missing); each time it opens, at the player's saved spot (`DB.meterPos`, its top left in UIParent's coordinates, dropped on load when not two finite numbers) or else anchored below the lowest shown game popup or the zone name text (ZoneTextFrame / SubZoneTextFrame, shown or not, so the zone-in name never sits under it), only read; never raised above them; nothing added to `StaticPopupDialogs`). Settings > Damage meter > Move (`M.Move`) closes the Settings panel and shows it to drag once the panel hides (`SP.CloseThen`), with Default (back to the automatic spot) / Lock (saves the spot); both reopen the Settings page; a prompt doesn't show while it's being moved (`/tgf meter` says so), and a question showing when Move starts is asked again when it ends; asking "[TGF] Reset Details! damage meter?" or "[TGF] Reset the damage meter?" (the plain `[TGF]` tag, as in the whispers); one at a time; nothing resets without Yes; Yes resets the meter the question named, and says so in chat when it's gone or the reset fails. `/tgf meter` shows it now
- `Messages.lua` — `ns.Messages`: the whisper presets (`invite` while you're in a group or to a solo player, `request` to a group leader while you're on your own) with placeholders (keys such as `{players}`; `{addon}` renders `[TGF]` and is always first and only once), defaults, Auto-send (off: prefill the whisper box, Enter to send) and Auto-invite (solo players: whisper, then invite by plain name; needs Auto-send), a 1 s contact lock after a send; where the preset uses `{activity}` and the listing has several activities, Whisper / Send Message first open the game's context menu at the cursor (`MenuUtil.CreateContextMenu`; the first activity where it's missing): one entry per activity (sent as the preset's Auto-send says; an entry does nothing while rows move and says so when a refresh or search took the listing away) and Edit (only fills the whisper box, every activity in `{activity}`, never sends or invites); and `BuildPanel`, which builds the Who-style Messages panel for both the game and the preview. `M.MarkInvited(l, preview)` is the one writer of `ns.state.invited` (Group Invite and Auto-invite): a per-invite table `{ name, at, outcome }`, shared by `Listings.Carry` so it follows the listing; outcomes come from `CHAT_MSG_SYSTEM` patterns built from the game's `ERR_*` format strings (joined / raid member added: accepted; declined, already in a group, ignoring you, player not found: declined; a decline 55 s or more after the invite: expired, the invitee's 60 s popup timeout), `GROUP_ROSTER_UPDATE` (the name in the party or raid: accepted) and a 2-minute timer (no answer: expired); matched by plain name; secret text skipped; the redraw waits while rows move; the preview resolves each invite after 2-4 s to accepted, declined, expired in turn; a new invite starts again at invited
- `Listings.lua` — `ns.Listings`: reads Forever's search results into listing tables (secret values skip the listing; a secret or empty zone or spec is dropped on its own), with friend / guildmate / recent ally marks, voice mode, playstyle and member counts; `ns.Listings.Read(previous, only)`: `only`, a set of activity IDs, leaves out listings with none of them (never yours)

- `UI/Table.lua` — `ns.Table`: the table placed in a Browse frame, scrolling in its own ScrollBox (Blizzard's `WowScrollBoxList` + `MinimalScrollBar`) with separate header and row frames (`W.List` fallback where the API is missing): sections, column headers above the list, selection by result ID (cleared on new results and when a search starts), the stock search states (spinner, no results, search failed, "no listings match" for our search box), the search box in the stone band beside the refresh button, Needs my role and the status in the bottom bar, a small icon hide button, Needs my role (a group that isn't full fits when the server's remaining slots or our estimate from its members' roles has a slot for one of your roles), sort within each section by Roles (T>H>D) / Lvl / Activity / Listed, friend marks, Whisper per row, right-click opens Forever's own row menu (`LFGBrowseFrame:CreateSearchEntryMenu`; a copy in the preview), Send Message + Group Invite at the bottom; rows show the solo player's or the leader's zone in grey at the start of the Activity cell's second line (capped, cut, before the comment; not on your own row), and a group's tooltip shows each member's zone as the line's right text (the contact buttons honour the 1 s lock and show the pressed look; Group Invite never locks).
